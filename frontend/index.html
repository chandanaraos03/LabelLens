Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011

Under Rule 6 of the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, every package must declare:

1. Net Quantity (Rule 6(1)(d))

Must be declared in standard SI metric units: mass (g, kg), volume (ml, l). Symbols like "gms", "ml.", or "kgs" are strictly non-compliant.

2. Maximum Retail Price (MRP)

Must include rupee symbol ₹ and explicit statement "(Inclusive of all taxes)".

3. Date of Mfg / Packing

Month and year of manufacture or packing (e.g. "08/2026").

4. Manufacturer Details

Complete legal entity name and complete physical postal address.

5. Consumer Care Helpline

Telephone number or verified email address for consumer grievances.

6. Country of Origin

Mandatory declaration of origin country.
